## Giving medication on schedule
Give prescribed medication at the same time every day, exactly as your veterinarian directed.
Medication tasks should be treated as high priority so they are never skipped when time is tight.
If a dose is missed, do not double the next dose; call your veterinarian for advice.
Never give a pet human medication (such as ibuprofen, acetaminophen, or naproxen) unless a veterinarian prescribes it.
TASK: Give prescribed medication | 08:00 | 5 | high | daily

## Flea, tick, and heartworm prevention
Most flea, tick, and heartworm preventives are given monthly or as labeled; set a recurring reminder.
Use only products made for your pet's species; some dog flea products are toxic to cats.
TASK: Flea and tick prevention | 09:00 | 5 | high | weekly

## Vet checkups and vaccines
Healthy adult pets should see a veterinarian at least once a year; seniors every six months.
Keep vaccination records up to date and schedule boosters as your vet recommends.
TASK: Vet checkup | 10:00 | 60 | medium | once
