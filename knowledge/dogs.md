## Exercise and walks
Most adult dogs need at least two walks a day, for a total of 30 to 60 minutes of activity.
High-energy breeds may need more; short-nosed breeds and seniors need shorter, gentler outings.
Avoid long walks in extreme heat and check pavement temperature with your hand.

TASK: Evening walk | 18:30 | 30 | medium | daily

## Feeding
Adult dogs usually eat two measured meals a day, about 8 to 12 hours apart.
Puppies under six months need three to four smaller meals a day.
Large, deep-chested dogs should rest for about an hour after a big meal before hard exercise, to lower the risk of bloat.
Fresh water should be available at all times; refresh the bowl at least once a day.
TASK: Breakfast | 07:45 | 10 | high | daily
TASK: Dinner | 17:30 | 10 | high | daily
TASK: Refresh water bowl | 12:00 | 5 | medium | daily

## Grooming and hygiene
Brush your dog at least once a week; long or double coats may need brushing several times a week.
Trim nails every three to four weeks, or when you hear them clicking on the floor.
Brushing teeth several times a week helps prevent dental disease.
TASK: Brush coat | 19:30 | 15 | low | weekly
TASK: Brush teeth | 20:00 | 5 | low | weekly

## Mental enrichment and training
Short training sessions of 5 to 15 minutes, once or twice a day, keep dogs mentally engaged.
Puzzle feeders, sniffing games, and fetch reduce boredom and destructive behavior.

TASK: Play fetch | 16:30 | 15 | low | daily
