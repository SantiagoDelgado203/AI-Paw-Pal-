## Feeding
Adult cats do well with two or more measured meals a day; kittens need three to four meals.
Cats are obligate carnivores and need a complete, balanced cat food, not dog food.
Many cats drink little, so a water fountain or wet food can help hydration. Refresh water daily.

TASK: Dinner | 18:00 | 5 | high | daily
TASK: Refresh water | 12:00 | 5 | medium | daily

## Litter box
Scoop the litter box at least once a day and fully change the litter about once a week.
A good rule is one litter box per cat plus one extra.
Changes in litter habits can signal a health problem and are worth mentioning to a vet.
TASK: Scoop litter box | 08:00 | 5 | high | daily
TASK: Full litter change | 10:00 | 20 | medium | weekly

## Play and enrichment
Indoor cats benefit from two interactive play sessions of 10 to 15 minutes a day.
Wand toys that mimic prey, scratching posts, and window perches reduce stress and boredom.
TASK: Interactive play | 19:00 | 15 | medium | daily
TASK: Morning play | 08:30 | 10 | low | daily

## Grooming
Short-haired cats should be brushed about once a week; long-haired cats need brushing daily to prevent mats.
Trim claws every two to three weeks if the cat does not wear them down on a scratching post.
TASK: Brush fur | 20:00 | 10 | low | weekly
