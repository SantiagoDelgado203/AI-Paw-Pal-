## Diet
Hay should make up most of a rabbit's diet and be available at all times.
Offer a portion of fresh leafy greens daily and only a small amount of pellets.
Fruit and carrots are treats and should be given sparingly.

TASK: Fresh greens | 18:00 | 10 | high | daily
TASK: Refresh water | 08:10 | 5 | high | daily

## Exercise and housing
Rabbits need several hours of supervised exercise outside the enclosure every day.
Rabbit-proof the area by covering cords and removing toxic plants.
TASK: Supervised free-roam time | 17:00 | 60 | medium | daily

## Grooming and hygiene
Never bathe a rabbit in water; it can cause shock. Spot-clean instead.
Brush weekly, and daily during heavy shedding. Trim nails every four to six weeks.
Clean the litter area daily and change bedding weekly.
TASK: Clean litter area | 08:20 | 10 | medium | daily
TASK: Brush rabbit | 19:00 | 10 | low | weekly
