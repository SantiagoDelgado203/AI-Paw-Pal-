## Diet, food, and water
What should a pet bird or parrot eat? Seed-only diets are unbalanced, so offer pellets plus fresh vegetables.
Replace food and water daily.
Remove uneaten fresh food after a few hours so it does not spoil.
TASK: Fresh food and water | 07:30 | 10 | high | daily
TASK: Remove leftover fresh food | 12:30 | 5 | medium | daily

## Social time and enrichment
Parrots and other social birds need daily interaction and time out of the cage, often one to two hours.
Rotate toys weekly to keep the bird engaged.
TASK: Out-of-cage social time | 17:00 | 60 | medium | daily
TASK: Rotate cage toys | 10:00 | 10 | low | weekly

## Cage hygiene and air safety
Change the cage liner daily and deep-clean the cage weekly.
Birds have very sensitive lungs: fumes from non-stick (PTFE) cookware, aerosols, and smoke can be fatal.
TASK: Change cage liner | 08:00 | 10 | medium | daily
TASK: Deep-clean cage | 11:00 | 30 | medium | weekly
