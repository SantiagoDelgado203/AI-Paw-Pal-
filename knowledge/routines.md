---
title: Building a Daily Routine
species: all
---

## Consistency
Pets feel safer with predictable routines: feed, walk, and medicate at roughly the same times each day.
Spread meals evenly through the day and schedule medication next to a meal if the vet says to give it with food.

## Prioritizing when time is short
When the day is busy, keep the essentials first: medication, food, water, and hygiene (like litter or potty breaks).
Enrichment and grooming can move to a less busy day, but should not be skipped for long.
Avoid stacking two tasks at the same time; you cannot walk the dog and clean the litter box at once.
