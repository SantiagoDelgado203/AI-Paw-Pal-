## Toxic foods and substances
Chocolate, xylitol (a sweetener in gum and some peanut butters), grapes and raisins, onions, garlic, alcohol, and caffeine are toxic to dogs and cats.
Lilies are extremely toxic to cats; even pollen or vase water can cause kidney failure.
Avocado and non-stick cookware fumes are dangerous for birds.
If a pet eats something toxic, contact a veterinarian or an animal poison control line right away.

## Emergency warning signs
Seek emergency veterinary care immediately for: trouble breathing, seizures, collapse, uncontrolled bleeding, suspected poisoning, a swollen hard belly with unproductive retching, inability to urinate, or severe vomiting or diarrhea.
Do not wait to see if these signs improve. Call your vet or the nearest emergency clinic.

## Heat and cold
Never leave a pet in a parked car; temperatures rise dangerously within minutes.
Signs of heatstroke include heavy panting, drooling, and weakness; move the pet somewhere cool and call a vet.
