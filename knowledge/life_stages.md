title: Puppies, Kittens, and Senior Pets
species: all

## Young animals
Puppies and kittens need more frequent, smaller meals: three to four a day until about six months old.
Young puppies need frequent potty breaks, especially after meals, naps, and play.
Early socialization and short, positive training sessions are important.
TASK: Midday meal | 12:30 | 10 | high | daily
TASK: Potty break | 10:30 | 10 | high | daily

## Senior pets
Dogs are often considered senior around 7 to 10 years old (earlier for giant breeds); cats around 11 years.
Seniors benefit from shorter, gentler, more frequent walks instead of one long outing.
Watch for stiffness, weight change, and changes in thirst, and schedule vet checkups every six months.
TASK: Gentle short walk | 12:00 | 15 | medium | daily
TASK: Senior vet checkup | 10:00 | 60 | medium | once
